Master’s thesis · University of Florence · 2025

Teaching a robotic hand from human demonstrations.

I built a pipeline to teach the Shadow Hand to grasp, hold and rotate a cube. It connects Leap Motion teleoperation, demonstration data, a learned policy and control on the physical hand.

01 Capture

Map finger and wrist features from Leap Motion to 22 joint commands. Apply joint limits, smoothing and rate limits before sending commands to the hand at approximately 100 Hz.

02 Learn

Record state–action sequences and train a recurrent Behavior Cloning policy. A GRU uses recent observation history to produce temporally coherent joint commands.

03 Execute

Estimate the cube’s pose with ArUco markers and combine the learned policy with control strategies. Test grasp stability and object rotation on the physical system.

What the experiments showed

The best physical trials achieved about two complete cube rotations in just over three minutes while maintaining the grasp. Results varied between trials: marker occlusion, latency and differences between simulated and real contact dynamics remained important limitations.

Control architecture and implementation details

On hardware, I used a compact six-feature state that includes object position, tilt, rotation speed and marker confidence. Quaternion filtering and temporary-occlusion handling reduced disruption from noisy or missing measurements.

I compared Behavior Cloning, a greedy one-step controller and their combination in simulation. For the physical hand, I implemented predictive control with Cross-Entropy Method sampling, joint masks and finger-dependent blending with the learned policy.

02 / Robotics laboratory · Team project · 2024

Franka Panda: perception and pick-and-place.

In a team project, I developed Python ROS nodes for keyboard teleoperation of a 7-DOF Panda. We used the physical robot to pick and place four cubes and integrated camera-based marker and colour recognition.

Python · ROS · Franka Panda · OpenCV · ArUco · HSV segmentation

What I implemented
Cartesian linear and angular velocity commands, gripper-parameter tuning, detection with the ArUco 6x6_1000 dictionary, and colour segmentation using tuned HSV thresholds. The setup demonstrated keyboard-driven manipulation and visual recognition.

03 / Automatic control · Team project · 2024

From a dynamic model to flight tests.

We modelled a quadrotor in Simulink, tuned cascaded attitude and angular-rate PID controllers, then mapped the gains to Betaflight. I worked on comparing simulation responses with flight logs and investigating drift in the logging IMU.

MATLAB · Simulink · Cascaded PID · Betaflight · IMU data analysis

Model and experimental findings
The nonlinear 6-DOF model included motor response, thrust and torque mixing, saturation and sensor noise. Flight responses were comparable to the default tuning. Bench tests helped isolate a sensor-estimation issue; adjusting complementary-filter weighting improved agreement with the flight-controller IMU.

04 / Multibody simulation · Team project

A hydraulically actuated gripper.

We built a parameterised model of a gripper for handling a test tube, coupling the linkage to a hydraulic cylinder and a feedback loop. The project examined how friction, contact stiffness and solver settings affect grasping and numerical stability.

MATLAB · Simulink · Simscape Multibody · Hydraulic actuation · Contact modelling

Simulation approach
We modelled sliding and gripper–object contacts, then refined an initial multiple-contact formulation with a simpler contact model and constrained object motion. The final model demonstrated the grasping mechanism and made the trade-off between contact detail and solver stability explicit.
